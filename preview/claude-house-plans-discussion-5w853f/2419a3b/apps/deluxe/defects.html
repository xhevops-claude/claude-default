Rules agreed so far

  1. R1 — Levels are finished surfaces. A number in a terrain profile is the surface you walk or drive on. A slab's top is that number; its underside is 20 cm below.
  2. R2 — Walls sit on the cut face, flush. A wall beside a cut stands in the 30 cm the cut is widened by, on the hill side of the line, and every wall along one edge is one continuous line.
  3. R3 — Nothing overlaps. A wall stops at a slab's underside; a slab stops at a wall's face. Two objects share a face, never a volume.
  4. R4 — Tangent joins. Where an arc meets a straight, the arc's end is tangent to the straight.
  5. R5 — One sampling. Anything that follows the ramp — slabs, walls, the ground grid — is sampled at the same stations.

Rules agreed so far

  1. R1 — Levels are finished surfaces. A number in a terrain profile is the surface you walk or drive on. A slab's top is that number; its underside is 20 cm below.
  2. R2 — Walls sit on the cut face, flush. A wall beside a cut stands in the 30 cm the cut is widened by, on the hill side of the line, and every wall along one edge is one continuous line.
  3. R3 — Nothing overlaps. A wall stops at a slab's underside; a slab stops at a wall's face. Two objects share a face, never a volume.
  4. R4 — Tangent joins. Where an arc meets a straight, the arc's end is tangent to the straight.
  5. R5 — One sampling. Anything that follows the ramp — slabs, walls, the ground grid — is sampled at the same stations.

Decisions

  • D1 — decided: the ramp starts at the house edge (z 6); the apron stays flat and whole.
  • D2 — decided: the driveway climbs from the very entrance; no flat pad. The mouth is the ramp's last 6 m and its floor is on the grade.
  • D3 — decided: a road-level landing. The ramp reaches the road at z 18; from there to the property's end the driveway is the road's own surface, and the outer wall runs out where the slab meets the road.
  • Site fact (survey): the parcel is 705 m², 44 m deep from the road, 19.3 m wide at the road and 10 m at the back, rising 17.8 m from its lowest road corner to the top corner. The frontage rises 1.6 m from its east corner to its south corner.
  • D5 — decided: the house sits 3.5 m further south within the parcel, inside the boundary. The surveyed ground is shown as the Relief group; the synthetic hill stays the model's terrain for now.
  • D4 — waiting: the grade is 22 % (27 % mid) because the surveyed frontage leaves 8 m for the ramp. Options: start the grade inside the apron, a shorter landing, a flatter ease, a different driveway layout, or accept it.
  • Site fact: the road is not level — it rises 1 m along the property, so the entrance (south end, z 22) is 2 m below the garage floor, not 3. Assumed: the north end of the property (z −2) is the 3 m-below end.

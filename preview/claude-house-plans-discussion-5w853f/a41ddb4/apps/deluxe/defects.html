Site facts

  • Parcels and DUP limits: 2.66 (615 m², footprint ≤ 187, built ≤ 749), 2.67.1 (398 / 174 / 696), 2.67.2 (342 / 138 / 551), 2.76 (185 / 68 / 271), 2.77 (293 / 122 / 489). All A1 housing, П+2+Пк, cornice 10.20 m.
  • Ground: 285.5–288.7 m along Rilski Kongres, a bank up to a terrace at 293–294 m, then 34 % up to 310 m at the west edge. The DUP's access street runs north–south on the west.
  • Buildings: a lower pair on 2.66 (Objekt 2) and 2.67.2 over one 942 m² garage at −2.50; an upper three on 2.67.1 (Objekt 1), 2.77 (Objekt 5) and 2.76, each with two basements dug into the slope. Story levels from the BIMx model; ±0.00 = 286.08 m from the profile drawing.

Rules carried over

  1. R1 — Levels are finished surfaces. A number in a terrain profile is the surface you walk or drive on. A slab's top is that number; its underside is 20 cm below.
  2. R2 — Walls sit on the cut face, flush. A wall beside a cut stands in the 30 cm the cut is widened by, on the hill side of the line, and every wall along one edge is one continuous line.
  3. R3 — Nothing overlaps. A wall stops at a slab's underside; a slab stops at a wall's face. Two objects share a face, never a volume.
  4. R4 — Tangent joins. Where an arc meets a straight, the arc's end is tangent to the straight.
  5. R5 — One sampling. Anything that follows a ramp — slabs, walls, the ground grid — is sampled at the same stations.

Each excavation is an object with its depth and volume on tap.
